| LD-25 | **Rows R2-V1, R2-V22, R2-V28, R2-V29. Dependents and stuck ops.** When a source op leaves `pending` without an ACK, its dependents take a terminal state with it, transitively: a `stuck` source makes them `failed(stuck)`, an `expired` source makes them `expired`, and any other failure, a discard, or a folded delete (which discards the send) makes them `failed(dependency_failed)`.
